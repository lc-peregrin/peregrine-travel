# Peregrin — Supply Outreach Batch (Version 2, compliant refundable/hold-fare model)

Replaces v1. v1's churn/no-show model asked agencies to create reservations with no ticketing intent and to keep volume "below enforcement attention" — that's an ask to breach their own GDS/IATA agreements, not a defensible commercial pitch. This version asks instead whether agencies can offer refundable fares or an official hold product, fully disclosing the use case.

**AA Travel and Anyways Travel were already sent the v1 (churn-model) email before this rewrite — see note in chat about a follow-up/correction for those two.** The other 16 below have not been contacted yet.

**Before sending:** verify missing emails (Global Travel Corp has none on file) via the company's contact form or a quick call first.

Volume would start modest (a few dozen bookings/month) and scale with demand on our side. I'm happy to structure this however keeps you clearly within your supplier agreements — including paying a service fee that reflects the extra handling, if that's the cleaner way to price it.
