## A. How it works (hero / above-the-fold supporting copy)

**Three steps, about a minute.**

1. **Search your route.** Enter where you're flying from and to, and your dates. We pull live fares
   directly from the airlines through Duffel.
2. **Hold a real reservation.** We create a genuine reservation with the airline in your name — a
   real booking reference (PNR), held, not ticketed. No airfare is charged.
3. **Get your document.** Download a clean, branded itinerary showing your reservation and booking
   reference — ready to show at check-in, immigration, or with a visa application. It's emailed to
   you too.

One flat fee of **US$14.99**. No airfare, no hidden charges, no card surcharge. If you later decide
you actually want to fly, you can confirm and pay for the ticket before the reservation expires.

## B. Why travellers trust Peregrin (trust block — 3 pillars)

**It's a real reservation, and you can prove it.**
Your booking reference is a genuine airline record, not a picture of one. Anyone — an airline agent,
an immigration officer, a visa officer — can check it directly against the airline's own system. A
verifiable reservation is the whole point; a screenshot that doesn't hold up isn't worth having.

**We're straight about what it is.**
It's a real *held reservation*, not a purchased ticket — and we say so plainly, on the document
itself. That honesty is exactly why it holds up: you're showing something real, described
accurately, not a claim that falls apart on a second look.

**It's fast.**
Search, hold, and document in about a minute. You get it on screen immediately and in your inbox.

## C. FAQ

**Is this legal?**
Yes. A held reservation is a standard, airline-sanctioned feature — airlines let bookings be held
before they're paid for and ticketed. We create that hold directly with the airline through Duffel,
their official booking platform. We don't forge documents or fake booking references; everything we
issue is a real record you can verify. What you do with a genuine reservation is your
responsibility, and requirements differ by country and situation — so we're transparent about
exactly what the document is, and isn't, so you can use it appropriately.

**Will it be accepted at check-in / immigration / for my visa?**
It's a real, verifiable reservation, which is what "proof of onward travel" checks are asking for —
and it's designed to stand up to a genuine look, because it *is* genuine. That said, we can't speak
for every individual airline agent, border officer, or embassy, and rules vary by destination and
change over time. We won't promise you a specific officer's decision — anyone who does is
overpromising. What we can promise is that what you're showing is authentic and independently
checkable, and that if we ever fail to deliver a valid, verifiable reservation, you're refunded in
full (see the refund policy below).

**How is this different from a "fake" or edited ticket?**
Completely. A fake ticket is an edited image with a booking reference that leads nowhere — it fails
the moment someone checks it, and using one can get you turned away or worse. Peregrin issues a
*real* reservation with a *real* booking reference held with the actual airline. You're not
carrying a convincing picture; you're carrying the genuine article.

**What exactly do I get?**
A held airline reservation in your name with a real booking reference, plus a clean branded itinerary
document (PDF) you can print or show on your phone. The document states clearly that it's a held
reservation. You also get a link to verify the reservation's live status at any time.

**How do I know — and show — that it's real?**
Every reservation has a live "verify" status you can check against the airline's own record. It's
the difference between "trust us" and "check for yourself." If you're ever asked whether the booking
is genuine, you (or the person asking) can confirm it directly.

**How long is the reservation valid? When does it expire?**
It's held until the airline's own payment deadline for that fare, then it lapses automatically if you
do nothing — you don't need to cancel anything, and you're never charged the airfare. The exact hold
window depends on the airline and route; it's shown on your document and on the confirmation screen
so you always know your window.

**What if I actually want to fly?**
Then you're in a great spot — the reservation is already made. Before it expires, you can confirm and
pay the airfare and it becomes a real ticket. You're never obligated to; most travellers just let the
hold lapse.

**Do you charge me for the flight?**
No. You pay one flat **US$14.99** for the reservation and your document. The airfare is only ever
charged if *you* choose to confirm and fly. No airfare, no surprise fees, no card surcharge — the
price you see is the price you pay.

**How fast will I get it?**
Right away — you'll have your document on screen within minutes, with a copy in your inbox. *(We
deliberately say "in minutes" rather than a hard "under 60 seconds" promise — it's true, it still
reads as fast, and it doesn't create an SLA to defend if an airline's system is briefly slow.)*

**What's the catch? How can it be this cheap?**
No catch — it's just a different product than a ticket. A held reservation costs us very little to
create because no airfare changes hands, so we can offer it for a small flat fee. You're paying for a
real, verifiable reservation and a professional document, delivered instantly — not for a seat on a
plane.

**What's your refund policy?**
Your **US$14.99 is a flat service fee** for creating and delivering a real, verifiable reservation —
issued in your name the moment you pay, with the document delivered immediately. Because it's a
genuine airline reservation made on the spot and handed to you right away, the fee isn't refundable
for a change of mind. You're protected where it counts: **if we can't create a valid, verifiable
reservation for you, you're refunded in full** — you never pay for something we didn't deliver. The
airfare is never charged for a reservation hold, so there's nothing else to refund. And if any detail
on your reservation is wrong because of a mistake on our end, tell us and we'll correct or reissue it
at no charge.

**Can I book a return, or for more than one person?**
Yes. You can hold a return or multi-city reservation (flat **US$19.99**), and add multiple travellers
— adults, children, and infants — in a single reservation.

**Can I get proof of accommodation too?**
That's coming — a real, freely-cancellable accommodation reservation for travellers who also need
proof of where they're staying. ⚠️ *(Gated on Duffel Stays access — keep this copy out of the live

**Is my payment secure?**
Yes. Payments are processed by Stripe, a global payment provider trusted by millions of businesses.
We never see or store your card details.

- Flat-price, no-surcharge wording is deliberate and compliance-driven (`BUSINESS_PLAN.md` §9) —
  don't add itemised fees or card surcharges.
- **Refund-policy rationale (internal, not customer-facing):** the policy is deliberately
  delivery-based (full refund only if we fail to deliver a valid, verifiable reservation), not
  acceptance-based. Grounded in how Stripe and Duffel actually work: Stripe does **not** return its
  processing fee on a refunded charge (the merchant eats it), and Duffel's per-order fee is a cost
  incurred at the reservation, not recovered on cancellation — so a change-of-mind refund on a
  delivered reservation is a direct loss, while acceptance is genuinely outside our control and
  guaranteeing it (as some competitors do) is a real liability. Reflect these in the Terms/Refund
  page too, consistent with this FAQ answer.
- **Economics flag for Liam (not copy):** in the current flow the Duffel hold order is created at the
  `/api/hold` step, *before* the customer pays the fee at `/api/order/:id/hold-checkout`. If Duffel
  bills its per-order fee at hold creation (as `BUSINESS_PLAN.md` §9 states), then holds that are
  created but never paid for still cost a few dollars each. Worth confirming with Claude Code, and if
  true, considering whether to create the Duffel hold only *after* the fee is paid.
