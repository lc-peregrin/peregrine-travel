Questions, answered plainly

Is this legal?–

Yes. A held reservation is a standard, airline-sanctioned feature — airlines let bookings be held before they're paid for and ticketed. We create that hold directly with the airline through Duffel, their official booking platform. We don't forge documents or fake booking references; everything we issue is a real record you can verify. What you do with a genuine reservation is your responsibility, and requirements differ by country — so we're transparent about exactly what the document is, and isn't.
